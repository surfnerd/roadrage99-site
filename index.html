ROAD RAGE

Get there. By any means necessary.

Get the game How to play

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. Floor it from A to B through fifteen chaotic levels: desert highways, rush-hour downtown, a rainy neon night, a mountain pass, the school run. Getting hit, stuck in traffic or leaning on your powers fills your ROAD RAGE meter. Unleash it and smash everything in your way, or sit on it and your heart gives out. Hit a car hard enough and its doors, hood and wheels go flying.

What you get

15 levels

Desert Highway, Downtown, Suburbs, Night City, Roadworks, Harbor, Mountain Pass, School Run, Late for Work, Pizza Delivery, Street Race, Golden Gate Park, Central Park, Leonardo and the Pursuit survival mode. Each one has a goal, and the goals are angry: GET TO THE D#%@ DENTIST!

One ROAD RAGE meter

Getting hit, rammed or stuck behind traffic fills it, and so does leaning on your powers. When it is full, UNLEASH IT: a few seconds of smashing cars out of your way. Wait too long and the chest pains start.

Cars come apart

Smash or slam a car hard enough and its doors, hood, trunk and wheels tear off, in every level. A light bump just leaves a dent. Ram or smash the same driver three times and they become your rival.

The Gauntlet

Finish a night level still raging and you never go to bed angry: the Gauntlet follows. A nightmare road with no retry and no music, just your heart pounding. Survive it and the garage cot lets you go back.

Alleys and shortcuts

Back alleys cut through the city levels, each with its own little scene, and the cops follow you in. Levels hide risky secret shortcuts too, like the jump over the marina in Leonardo.

Powers and cops

Grab crates for the Juggernaut, Ghost Car, Nitro, Ramp Plow, Mech Bat and the rare THE BARREL. Drive like a maniac and the cops pull you over: mash TALK to get out of it fast. Push your luck and a second cop joins the chase.

A new look every run

Over 40 lighting looks across the levels: neon nights with lit windows, rain-slicked roads, fog, golden hour and dusk skies. Same road, different mood, every time you play.

The inventor's garage

Your home base. Pick a level off the cork board, buy and tune cars from the lift's '70s speed-shop catalog, and watch your lifetime smash tally grow on the bench. Every finish earns Gas Money.

7 cars to drive

The all-rounder Drifter, the fast Rocket, the tough Boulder, the Hauler, the Muscle and the Taxi, plus one money can't buy: spot Capn Snafu in every level to earn his car. Upgrade rage, suspension, speed, toughness and steering.

Stunt Park

Finish every level and a free-drive playground opens: a loop-the-loop, flaming hoops, a bus jump, car stacks to plow through and a banked oval. No clock, no cops, just stunts.

Achievements

25 achievements (two of them hidden) and best-time leaderboards through Game Center on iPhone and iPad and Google Play Games on Android. Signed in, your progress follows you from device to device.

Built for phone and pad

Gesture controls: hold to drive, steer with your thumb, double-tap to smash. Bluetooth gamepads work too, and so does a keyboard. The Practice Lot teaches you the ropes on your first drive.
