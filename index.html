Get the game How to play

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. Floor it from A to B through fifteen chaotic levels: desert highways, rush-hour downtown, a rainy neon night, a mountain pass, the school run. Shove through traffic, take the secret shortcuts, and try not to make the cops, or your own heart, too angry.

What you get

15 levels

From the Desert Highway to Downtown, Night City, Harbor, Mountain Pass, Pizza Delivery, Street Race and the Pursuit survival mode.

6 cars and a garage

The all-rounder Drifter, the fast Rocket, the tough Boulder, the Hauler, the Muscle and the Taxi. Earn Gas Money, buy cars and upgrade speed, steering, suspension and toughness.

Built for phone and pad

Touch controls out of the box, plus gamepad support, including Bluetooth controllers.
