ROAD RAGE

Get there. By any means necessary.

Get the game How to play

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. Floor it from A to B through fifteen chaotic levels: desert highways, rush-hour downtown, a rainy neon night, a mountain pass, the school run. Getting hit or stuck in traffic fills your ROAD RAGE meter. Unleash it and smash everything in your way, or sit on it and your heart gives out. Every run picks a new look, from dusk skies to neon-soaked wet streets.

What you get

15 levels

From the Desert Highway to Downtown, Night City, Harbor, Mountain Pass, Pizza Delivery, Street Race, Golden Gate Park and the Pursuit survival mode. Each one has a goal, and the goals are angry: GET TO THE D#%@ DENTIST!

One ROAD RAGE meter

Getting hit, rammed or stuck behind traffic fills it. When it is full, UNLEASH IT: a few seconds of smashing cars out of your way. Wait too long and the chest pains start.

A new look every run

Over 40 lighting looks across the levels: neon nights with lit windows, rain-slicked roads, fog, golden hour and dusk skies. Same road, different mood, every time you play.

6 cars and a garage

The all-rounder Drifter, the fast Rocket, the tough Boulder, the Hauler, the Muscle and the Taxi. Earn Gas Money, buy cars and upgrade speed, steering, suspension and toughness.

Stunt Park

Finish every level and a free-drive playground opens: a loop-the-loop, flaming hoops, a bus jump, car stacks to plow through and a banked oval. No clock, no cops, just stunts.

Achievements

More than 20 achievements and best-time leaderboards through Game Center on iPhone and iPad and Google Play Games on Android. Each achievement has its own badge.

Powers and cops

Grab crates for the Juggernaut, Ramp Plow, Mech Bat, Parting of the Sea and more. Drive like a maniac and the cops pull you over: talk your way out fast.

Built for phone and pad

Gesture controls out of the box (hold to drive, double-tap to smash), classic buttons if you prefer, and gamepad support, including Bluetooth controllers. The Practice Lot teaches you the ropes on your first drive.
