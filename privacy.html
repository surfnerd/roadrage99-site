Privacy policy

This policy explains what the Road Rage game for iPhone and iPad (“the game”) collects and how it is used. Questions: support@roadrage99.com.

The short version: there are no accounts, we do not ask for your name, email, contact list, location, camera or microphone. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads, and the ad service may collect some data, as described below. Anonymous play stats are off until you say yes.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, best times, selected car, settings, whether you own Remove Ads, and your answers to the questions below) is saved locally in the game's storage on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the game deletes it.

2. Ads

The game is free and shows two kinds of ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch to start a run with a power, and interstitial ads that can appear between runs. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (a mediation service from Unity, which includes the ironSource and Unity Ads networks) and the ad networks it connects. When an ad is requested or shown, these companies may collect information such as your device's IP address, device and operating system details, the ad you saw and how you interacted with it, and, if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). They use it to choose and show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, depending on your choice, personalize ads.

Tracking question (iOS). On first launch iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the game does not get your advertising identifier and ads still appear, but they are less relevant. You can change this any time in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

The ad companies act on their own behalf for some of this data. Their policies: ironSource / Unity LevelPlay and Unity.

You can remove between-run ads with the Remove Ads purchase (section 4).

3. Anonymous play stats (optional, off until you say yes)

The first time you open the menu, the game asks “help improve Road Rage?”. Nothing is sent until you tap yes. If you do, the game uses Unity Analytics to record anonymous events so we can tune difficulty and see where players get stuck:

  • when you start, finish, fail or quit a level, with the level name and number, the car you picked, whether Free Drive was on, how many seconds you played, how far you got, your stars (on a win), why you failed (on a loss), the power you held (if any), and whether you quit to the menu or to retry;
  • when you watch an ad (rewarded or between runs) and whether you earned the reward;
  • when you buy Remove Ads.

These events are not tied to your name or Apple ID. Unity Analytics assigns a random player identifier to your install and, as part of the service, may also receive technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, language, time zone and approximate country from your IP address. Check: confirm exactly what Unity Analytics collects automatically against Unity's current docs.

You can turn this off any time: menu, options, Share Stats. Turning it off stops collection and asks Unity to delete the data already sent for your install.

4. Purchases

The only purchase is Remove Ads (one time, not a subscription), made through Apple's In-App Purchase using Unity IAP. Apple handles your payment; we never see your card or billing details. The game checks with the App Store whether you own the purchase (including for Restore Purchases) and remembers it on your device. If you opt in to play stats, we also record that a purchase happened, without payment details.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, and the game has no accounts, chat or social features. We do not sell your data. We do not use the web analytics or tracking scripts on this website; see section 8.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us information, email us and we will delete it. Check: age rating, App Store “Made for Kids” status and whether the game sets the ad networks' child-directed and age flags. Any ad-supported game with kids as players needs this settled.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Say no to tracking in the iOS prompt, or change it in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off Share Stats in the game's options, which also requests deletion.
  • Reset Limit Ad Tracking or your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop between-run ads.
  • Email support@roadrage99.com to ask what we hold, or to ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or object. Because we cannot identify you from the anonymous stats, we may ask for details such as your install's date and device to find them. Check: GDPR/CCPA wording, and whether a consent prompt for EU/UK players is required for ad personalization.

8. This website

This site is plain pages: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no external fonts or CDNs. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitor IP addresses as any web host does (GitHub's privacy statement). If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will change the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the game or on this site.
