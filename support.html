FAQ

Where can I get Road Rage?

On the App Store for iPhone and iPad in Canada and the Philippines, where it is called Road Rage '99. We are starting with these two countries, and more come later. On Android it is in testing on Google Play; see Android testing below.

How do I reverse?

Touch: pull the stick down to brake, and keep it down at a stop to back up.

Gamepad: hold the left trigger (LT on Xbox, L2 on PlayStation). Keyboard: hold S.

What are the controls?

On a touch screen the controls are gestures. Plug in or pair a controller, or press a key on a keyboard, and it takes over from the touch controls. To see the controls in the game, open Options (top right), then CONTROLS, then HOW TO PLAY, or tap HELP in the pause menu.

Touch (gestures)

  • Steer: put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side and the stick appears under it. Pull it down to brake.
  • Gas: hold a thumb anywhere on the right side. Lift it to coast.
  • Smash: double-tap the right side. Flick the second tap to aim it.
  • Power: holding a power? Slide your gas thumb up onto POWER. The gas stays on.
  • Switch power: a quick slide right with your gas thumb swaps in your NEXT power.
  • Rage: when the ROAD RAGE meter fills, POWER turns into RAGE. Slide up onto it.
  • Pulled over: mash TALK to lose less time.

Controller

  • Left stick: steer. Right trigger (RT / R2): gas. Left trigger (LT / L2): brake, and back up at a stop.
  • B / Circle: handbrake. D-pad: smash ahead, back, left or right.
  • X / Square: use your power, or unleash RAGE. Bumpers (LB / RB): switch power.
  • A / Cross: mash TALK when you are pulled over. Menu button: pause.

Keyboard

  • W: gas. A / D: steer. S: brake, and back up at a stop. Space: handbrake.
  • Arrow keys: smash ahead, back, left or right. X: use your power, or unleash RAGE. Z / C: switch power.
  • F: mash TALK when you are pulled over. Esc: pause.
What is the ROAD RAGE meter?

It is your one meter. Getting hit, ramming other cars, sitting stuck behind traffic and using powers fill it. When it is full, POWER turns into RAGE: slide up onto it (or press X / Square, or X on a keyboard) to UNLEASH IT. For a few seconds cars bounce off you, your smash hits much harder and you go faster, but the cops notice. If you sit on a full meter, the chest pains start, then a heart attack strike. Three heart attacks and the run is over. A Chill Pill empties the meter.

What do the other meters mean?
  • MENACE: reckless driving fills it. When it is full, the cops come after you. Outrun them or smash them.
  • CAR: your car's health. Hits dent the car and slow it down. A Duct Tape power fixes it.
  • BUSTED: the lights count your pull-overs. The third one busts you.
  • HEART: sit on a full ROAD RAGE and the chest pains come, then a heart attack. Three heart attacks and the run is over.

You can see these in the game too: Options, CONTROLS, HOW TO PLAY, then the METERS tab.

How do I get three stars?

Each level card shows the times for two and three stars. Three stars also needs a clean run: no pull-overs and no heart attacks. After a run, the results screen shows each star's target and why you missed the next one.

What is the Gauntlet?

A nightmare. Finish a night level with your ROAD RAGE still high and you never go to bed angry: the Gauntlet follows. There is no retry. Survive it and a cot appears in the garage, so you can go back any time.

Why does a level look different every time?

Each run picks one of several lighting looks for the level: a neon night with lit windows, rain and wet roads, fog, golden hour or a dusk sky. It changes how the level looks, not how it plays: your best times count across every look.

Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Your first drive starts in the Practice Lot. To go back any time, pick the Practice Lot photo (the one with the START HERE sticky) on the cork board. To see the intro again, open Options, then GAME, then PLAY INTRO; RESET TIPS there brings back the first-time hints.

Does it work with a controller or a keyboard?

Yes. Connect a Bluetooth gamepad (Xbox or PlayStation controllers pair in iOS Settings, then Bluetooth, or in Android Settings, then Connected devices) and the touch controls give way to controller prompts. A keyboard works too; pressing a key hides the touch controls. The game keeps your screen awake while you play, so it will not lock mid-race.

Can I turn off vibration?

Yes: Options, then CONTROLS, then VIBRATION switches every haptic and controller rumble on or off. REDUCE MOTION is on the same page.

How do I restore my purchases?

Cars and Remove Ads come back with Restore Purchases. Gas Money packs and Super Juggernaut starts are consumables: once spent, they do not restore. To get your cars and Remove Ads back on a new phone or after a reinstall: open Options (top right), then ACCOUNT, then RESTORE PURCHASES. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone and iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought them with. If it says nothing was found, check that you are signed in with the right account, then email us.

What can I buy?

Gas Money packs, cars, Super Juggernaut starts and Remove Ads. Everything you can buy with real money you can also earn with Gas Money from finishing levels, except Super Juggernaut starts and Remove Ads. Prices vary by country; the store page shows what is on offer where you are.

What does Remove Ads remove?

The occasional ad shown between runs. Optional rewarded ads (watch one to start a run with a power) are always your choice. See the privacy policy for how ads work.

The game asked about tracking and about sharing stats. What are those?

The first is Apple's standard tracking question for ads: saying no still lets ads run, just less personalized. The second is our one-time "help improve Road Rage?" question for anonymous play stats. You can switch it off any time: Options, then GAME, then SHARE STATS. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the game also asks whether ads may be personalized; change that under Options, GAME, PERSONALIZED ADS. Details are in the privacy policy.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and in the cloud if you are signed in: through Game Center and iCloud on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play Games on Android. Signed in, your stars, best times, cars and upgrades follow you to your other devices on the same account and come back after a reinstall. If you are not signed in, deleting the app deletes your progress.

I can't hear anything.

Check the ring/silent switch (iPhone) or your media volume (Android), and the AUDIO volume in Options. If it still happens, email us with your device and its iOS or Android version.

Beta testing (TestFlight and Google Play)

Testers get early builds before they reach the stores: expect rough edges. On iPhone and iPad we test through TestFlight, and on Android through Google Play testing. Tester builds have no between-run ads, so testers can just play. To report a bug, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback (screenshot and a note) or email support@roadrage99.com.

If your install code or invitation does not work, email us the address on your Apple ID and we will check the tester list.

Android testing

Road Rage is in testing on Google Play for Android. If you want to get on our testing list, reach out to support@roadrage99.com. These are early builds: expect rough edges. To report a bug, email us your phone model, Android version and what happened.
