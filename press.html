Short description

Road Rage is a fast, chaotic driving game for your phone. You are late; get from A to B before the clock runs out, by any means necessary.

Long description

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. In Road Rage you floor it from A to B through fifteen levels: an open desert highway, rush-hour downtown, a rain-soaked neon night, a stormy harbor, a mountain pass, the school run, a pizza delivery and a street race, each with a goal shouted at you (GET TO THE D#%@ DENTIST!). Every hit, ram and minute stuck behind traffic fills your ROAD RAGE meter. Unleash it and the cars bounce off you for a few glorious seconds; sit on it and the chest pains start, then your heart gives out. Grab powers like the Juggernaut, find the risky secret shortcuts, and watch the heat you attract, because the cops will pull you over. Every run picks a new lighting look, from neon nights with lit windows and wet roads to fog, golden hour and dusk skies. Earn Gas Money for every finish, then spend it in the garage on new cars (the Drifter, Rocket, Boulder, Hauler, Muscle and Taxi) and on upgrades for speed, steering, suspension and toughness. Gesture controls out of the box, classic buttons or a Bluetooth gamepad, and a Practice Lot to learn the ropes. A low-poly, late-90s look and a bouncy, grabby suspension make every crash feel good.

Features

  • Fifteen levels, from the Desert Highway and Downtown to Night City, Harbor, Mountain Pass, Pizza Delivery, Street Race, Golden Gate Park and the Pursuit survival mode.
  • One ROAD RAGE meter: fill it by getting hit or stuck, then UNLEASH IT, or your heart gives out.
  • Over 40 lighting looks picked at random each run: neon, lit windows, rain-slicked roads, fog, sunsets and dusk skies.
  • Powers such as the Juggernaut, Ramp Plow and Parting of the Sea; cops who pull you over; secret shortcuts.
  • The Stunt Park, a free-drive playground with a loop-the-loop, flaming hoops and a bus jump, opens when you finish every level.
  • Achievements and leaderboards through Game Center (iPhone and iPad) and Google Play Games (Android).
  • Six cars and a garage: earn Gas Money by finishing levels, buy cars and upgrade speed, steering, suspension and toughness.
  • Gesture touch controls by default, classic buttons, and gamepad support, including Bluetooth controllers. A Practice Lot tutorial on your first drive.
