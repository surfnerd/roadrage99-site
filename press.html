Short description

Road Rage is a fast, chaotic driving game for your phone. You are late; get from A to B before the clock runs out, by any means necessary.

Long description

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. In Road Rage you floor it from A to B through twelve levels: an open desert highway, rush-hour downtown, a rain-soaked neon night, a mountain pass, the school run, a pizza delivery and a street race. Shove through traffic, find the risky secret shortcuts, and keep an eye on your own rage and the heat you attract, because the cops notice and your heart is not as young as it was. Pick one of three cars with different speed, toughness and grip, play with touch or a Bluetooth gamepad, and chase three stars on every level. A low-poly, late-90s look and a bouncy, grabby suspension make every crash feel good.
