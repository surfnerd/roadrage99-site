Short description

Road Rage is a fast, chaotic driving game for your phone. You are late; get from A to B before the clock runs out, by any means necessary.

Long description

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. In Road Rage you floor it from A to B through fifteen levels: an open desert highway, rush-hour downtown, a rain-soaked neon night, a stormy harbor, a mountain pass, roadworks, the school run, a pizza delivery and a street race, each with a goal shouted at you (GET TO THE D#%@ DENTIST!). Every hit, ram and minute stuck behind traffic fills your ROAD RAGE meter, and so does every power you use. Unleash it and the cars bounce off you for a few glorious seconds; sit on it and the chest pains start, then your heart gives out. Smash a car hard enough and its doors, hood, trunk and wheels tear off. Grab powers like the Juggernaut and the rare THE BARREL, duck through back alleys, find the risky secret shortcuts, and watch the heat you attract, because the cops will pull you over. Finish a night level still raging and you will meet the Gauntlet, a nightmare drive with no retry. Every run picks a new lighting look, from neon nights with lit windows and wet roads to fog, golden hour and dusk skies. Between runs you are in the inventor's garage: pick levels off the cork board, buy cars from a '70s speed-shop catalog (the Drifter, Rocket, Boulder, Hauler, Muscle and Taxi, plus one you can only earn) and upgrade their rage, suspension, speed, toughness and steering with the Gas Money you earn. Gesture controls, Bluetooth gamepads or a keyboard, and a Practice Lot to learn the ropes. A low-poly, late-90s look and a bouncy, grabby suspension make every crash feel good.

Features

  • Fifteen levels: Desert Highway, Downtown, Suburbs, Night City, Roadworks, Harbor, Mountain Pass, School Run, Late for Work, Pizza Delivery, Street Race, Golden Gate Park, Central Park, Leonardo and the Pursuit survival mode.
  • One ROAD RAGE meter: fill it by getting hit, getting stuck or using powers, then UNLEASH IT, or your heart gives out.
  • Cars come apart: smashes and hard hits tear off doors, hoods, trunks and wheels in every level.
  • The Gauntlet, a nightmare bonus level for drivers who finish a night level still raging.
  • Back alleys with their own little scenes in the city levels, and risky secret shortcuts.
  • Over 40 lighting looks picked at random each run: neon, lit windows, rain-slicked roads, fog, sunsets and dusk skies.
  • Powers such as the Juggernaut, Ghost Car, Nitro, Ramp Plow, Mech Bat and THE BARREL; cops who pull you over and chase you down.
  • The inventor's garage: a cork-board level select, a '70s speed-shop catalog for cars and upgrades, and a lifetime smash tally.
  • Seven cars: six to buy with the Gas Money you earn, and Capn Snafu's own car for spotting him in every level.
  • The Stunt Park, a free-drive playground with a loop-the-loop, flaming hoops and a bus jump, opens when you finish every level.
  • 25 achievements and best-time leaderboards through Game Center (iPhone and iPad) and Google Play Games (Android), with progress synced through iCloud or Google Play Games.
  • Gesture touch controls, gamepad support including Bluetooth controllers, keyboard support, and a Practice Lot tutorial on your first drive.
