Short description

Road Rage is a fast, chaotic driving game for your phone. You are late; get from A to B before the clock runs out, by any means necessary.

Long description

You are late, the traffic is slow and the clock is not on your side. In Road Rage you floor it from A to B through fifteen levels: an open desert highway, rush-hour downtown, a rain-soaked neon night, a mountain pass, the school run, a pizza delivery and a street race. Shove through traffic, find the risky secret shortcuts, and keep an eye on your own rage and the heat you attract, because the cops notice and your heart is not as young as it was. Earn Gas Money for every finish, then spend it in the garage on new cars (the Drifter, Rocket, Boulder, Hauler, Muscle and Taxi) and on upgrades for speed, steering, suspension and toughness, and watch the garage swap each rusty part for a shiny one. Play with touch or a Bluetooth gamepad, and chase three stars on every level. A low-poly, late-90s look and a bouncy, grabby suspension make every crash feel good.

Features

  • Fifteen levels, from the Desert Highway and Downtown to Night City, Harbor, Mountain Pass, Pizza Delivery, Street Race and the Pursuit survival mode.
  • Six cars: Drifter, Rocket, Boulder, Hauler, Muscle and Taxi.
  • The garage: earn Gas Money by finishing levels, buy cars and upgrade speed, steering, suspension and toughness.
  • The upgrade show: the car lifts, goes X-ray, and the rusty part is swapped for a shiny one.
  • Powers such as the Juggernaut, rage, menace and heart-attack stress, cops, and secret shortcuts.
  • Touch controls and gamepad support, including Bluetooth controllers.
